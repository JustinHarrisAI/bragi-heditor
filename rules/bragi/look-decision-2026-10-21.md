# Look decision, 2026-10-21 (Justin Harris, CEO). Read on every run. Overrides RULES.md and LOOK.md.

Earlier rounds were too constrained by the JHAI brand files. For Justin's personal reels the grip is loosened.

## Keep (still hard rules)
- Glow `#A6F0DA` is Justin's signature accent. Use it everywhere it helps, with NO per-frame limit. Where the
  reference reels use yellow (or any highlight colour), use Glow instead.
- Never cover Justin's face (eyes to chin) with text, graphics or cards.
- No em dashes or en dashes in any on-screen text.
- Natural-colour footage (no B&W, no heavy grade). B&W stays only for gym/mood clips via the mood template.

## Free (no longer rules for these reels)
Typefaces, caption style, layout and effects are free. Piazzolla/Geist, the Ink band, preset E captions,
"flat colour only" and "one Glow per frame" NO LONGER APPLY. Copy what works in the reference reels (Chase AI, Nate
Herk, Jack Roberts, tenfoldmarc): heavy sans display type, word-by-word pop captions, highlight boxes, shadows,
outlines, glows, gradients, 3D, whatever lands, with Glow in place of their yellow. Logos still only from
`looks/bragi/logo/` if a logo is used at all.

## Motion graphics: visualize every concept he says
Not just captions. Each concept becomes a real animated graphic, 3 to 6 per 50 s reel (scale for shorter reels):
- SEO: a ranking climbing a Google results page (his result rising to #1).
- Folder structure: an animated file tree building itself.
- Marketing: a funnel filling.
- An idea: nodes connecting.
- Numbers: animated counters.
- Data, landing pages, video, AI: draw them (a database plugging in, a landing page assembling, a play button,
  a chat window typing).
Use the installed libraries listed in `/Users/justinharris/code/jhai-wt-bragi/research/bragi-video-edit-2026-10/motion-libraries.md` (libraries in
`~/code/bragi-src/motion-libs/`) where they fit; build the rest in GSAP/SVG/three.js.

## Overshoot
Too crazy beats too refined. Forced is fine. Kinetic type slams, 3D moves, screen-filling graphics with Justin cut
out on top (cutout/matte layer over the graphic), whip moves, zoom slams, a pattern interrupt every 1 to 2 seconds.

## Two intensities per reel
- **wild**: maximum. Every trick, every beat, interrupt every ~1 s.
- **strong**: wild pulled back about 30% (fewer simultaneous elements, slightly calmer moves, interrupt every
  ~1.5 to 2 s), same graphics and structure. Strong must still pass the judge.
