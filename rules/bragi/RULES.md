# Bragi hard rules (Justin Harris personal reels)

These override every stock look, template default and reference habit. Source of truth: JHAI `brand/DESIGN.md`.
Break one and the reel is wrong, however good it looks.

## Colour
- Palette for type and graphics: Ink `#161B1C`, White `#FFFFFF`, white at 70% (`rgba(255,255,255,.70)`), Glow `#A6F0DA`.
  Nothing else. No butter yellow `#FAE67A`, no cream, no orange, no red, no blue, no teal tone overlay.
- Ink, never pure black. Anything dark (bands, end card, veils) is `#161B1C`.
- **One Glow element per frame, maximum.** A Glow key word, a Glow rule, a Glow dot are each one. Two Glow things on
  screen at the same moment is a failure. Glow is never on a face.
- Never Glow text on a light ground (a white card, a bright sky behind it). Glow sits on Ink or on dark footage.
- Flat colour only. No gradients, drop shadows, text shadows, outlines, strokes, glows, blooms, light sweeps,
  vignettes or blur halos on type or graphics. If legibility needs help, use the flat Ink band.
  (Motion blur during a 0.2s entrance is motion, not a style, and is allowed; type at rest is sharp.)

## Type
- **Big words:** Piazzolla Regular 400. The **key word** of a big line: Piazzolla Italic 600 in Glow (that frame's
  one Glow element). Tracking -0.02em to -0.03em, line height about 1.0.
- **Captions:** Geist SemiBold 600, sentence case, preset E "Quiet Operator": about 58px white on a flat Ink band at
  78% opacity, 24px top/bottom and 39px left/right padding, 13px corners, centred, band top near 68% of frame
  height (move it up or down only to keep it off the face and inside the safe zone), one phrase at a time grouped
  by meaning, words not yet spoken at 62% white and 100% as they are said, never break a word. A caption key word
  may switch to Piazzolla Italic 600 Glow only when no other Glow is on screen.
- **Small spaced caps (kickers, labels, units, index numbers):** Geist Mono 400, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.14em,
  white at 70%. Never at display size.
- Fonts come only from `looks/bragi/fonts/`. Never Inter, Inter Tight, Montserrat, DM Serif, Instrument Serif, Caveat.
- No em dashes or en dashes in any on-screen text. Use a full stop or a comma.
- Text is left-aligned or centred, never right-aligned.

## Footage
- Talking reels: natural colour. Only a light white-balance or exposure fix if the clip needs it. No B&W,
  no warm or teal grade, no crushed blacks, no film grain, no vignette.
- Black and white is only for gym/mood clips, and those go to the locked mood template, never this look.
- Never cover Justin's face (eyes to chin) with any text, band, card or insert. Behind-head words must stay
  readable (at least ~70% of each letter visible) and may tuck behind the top of the head only.

## Logo
- Only from `looks/bragi/logo/` (copied byte for byte from JHAI `brand/logo/`). White on Ink only.
- Never on a photograph or over footage, never Glow, never with an effect. If used at all: a short flat Ink end card.

## Words
- First person, Justin's words. Captions say what he actually said (fix Whisper mishearings: "Claude", "SEO", "AI").
- No hype words on screen. No fabricated numbers, no fake proof, no other creator's handle unless their reel is shown.

## Every talking reel gets the full produced look
Best takes, dead air cut, punch-ins, preset E captions all the way through, big Piazzolla words on the key lines
(some behind the head when the cutout allows), Geist Mono kickers, motion on every text entrance, subtle SFX under
the voice. No reel ships as "captions only".
